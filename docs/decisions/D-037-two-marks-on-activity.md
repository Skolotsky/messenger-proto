# D-037: Activity shows Focus and Needs you separately, and the second becomes a dot under Focus only

- Status: accepted
- Level: product
- Date: 2026-11-23
- Decided by: user
- Depends on: D-012, D-019. Supersedes the single number of D-036

## Why this needs deciding now

A single sum hides what kind of attention is waiting. The user asked for two numbers.

## Decision

With "Focus and Needs you", Activity shows two numbers in the colours of their sections. With "Focus only", the Needs you number becomes a quiet dot. A part with nothing unread shows nothing.

## Consequences

The sidebar, the phone's bottom bar and the Feed tab show the same mark. No contract changes.
