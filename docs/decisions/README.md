# Decisions

What has been decided and what is still open, from the top down. One line per decision; the record holds the reasoning. How decisions are prepared and recorded: the `decide` skill.

Status: `open` (identified, not yet prepared) · `proposed` (brief ready, waiting for the user) · `accepted` · `superseded by D-nnn`

Line format: `- **D-nnn** [status] The decision as a statement, and what it commits us to → [record](D-nnn-slug.md)`

## Product: what we build and for whom

- **D-001** [accepted] The early access programme tests one bet: the feed sorts what deserves attention into three buckets (Focus by the person's own rules, Needs you, For your information), tuned by a dial per channel and per thread and by the sender's Flag or Info. Commits us to explained rules, one item in one place, no jumping on edit or delete, and model-judged text rules built last. Flag closing superseded by D-011; Muted and All activity narrowed by D-013; the starting rule for direct messages superseded by D-021 → [record](D-001-product-hypothesis.md)
- **D-002** [accepted] People and agents in the first version are scripted and enter through the doors real ones would use; no model is needed to run it. A coding agent can join as an agent or on behalf of a person, as a demonstration feature for verification → [record](D-002-agents-in-first-version.md)
- **D-003** [accepted] Reading a row is enough for it to leave the feed; a flag stays until it closes, and anything the person keeps stays in Focus until they mark it done. Commits us to Keep and Done, an Earlier list, replying in place, and a list that does not move while viewed. How a flagged row leaves is superseded by D-011; the Earlier list is replaced by the history tabs (D-016); expanding a row in place and replying from it are dropped: a click opens the conversation (D-023) → [record](D-003-how-items-leave-the-feed.md)
- **D-004** [accepted] A deleted message leaves no trace in a conversation unless something depends on it (replies). In the feed, something taken back leaves a marker only where the person had already seen it, and one that stays for flagged or kept rows. An edit that adds a mention or flag reaches the person as new. The marker for a flagged row is superseded by D-011 → [record](D-004-edits-and-deletes.md)
- **D-009** [accepted] The first version is the feed plus what makes it observable: direct messages, a search box, a notification tray, a Focus rules list, unread marks, creating channels, and a layout that works on a phone-sized screen, delivered as an installable web app (PWA). The notification tray is superseded by D-022. Commits us to two widths for every surface; the access model, sign-in, group messages, ad-hoc topics, profiles, files and formatted text stay out → [record](D-009-first-version-surfaces.md)
- **D-010** [accepted] The seeded team is large: about 30 people, 6 agents and 20 channels, with a core cast carrying hand-written situations and the rest as background. A separate control panel starts, stops and resets the demonstration and shows what happened and what comes next → [record](D-010-seeded-team.md)
- **D-011** [accepted] A flag is a marker for each reader's feed, with no state of its own: one that names you is kept for you automatically until you press Done or respond; one that names nobody is an ordinary row in Needs you. Supersedes how a flag closes in D-001, D-003 and D-004. Commits us to no status on a flag anywhere → [record](D-011-a-flag-is-a-marker-for-the-readers-feed.md)
- **D-012** [accepted] Focus and Needs you send a notification, For your information never; one notification per row, not per message; one personal switch to "Focus only". Commits us to notifications following the bucket, with no notification settings per channel → [record](D-012-what-sends-a-notification.md)
- **D-013** [accepted] The readings E-001 needed: a muted channel does not silence your own threads; All activity leaves addressed items as at Default; a dial or rule change re-sorts unread rows; plus a list of smaller assumed behaviours that stand until objected to → [record](D-013-feed-rules-confirmed-after-e-001.md)
- **D-014** [accepted] A reaction from someone you addressed is an answer and lands in Needs you with their name; feed rows show the reactions on their message; the grouped row shows the split by emoji. No emoji has a built-in meaning → [record](D-014-reactions-in-the-feed.md)
- **D-015** [accepted] Read is one position per person and conversation, shared by the feed, channel unread marks and notifications on every device. Opening marks read in Focus and Needs you; acting implies read; nothing is read while the person is not looking; no mark-as-unread → [record](D-015-what-read-means.md)
- **D-016** [accepted] Besides the feed there are four history tabs for finding things again: Direct messages, Mentions, Threads, Reactions. They are not sorted by buckets or dials and nothing leaves them by being read → [record](D-016-history-tabs.md)
- **D-019** [accepted] The product is laid out like the work messenger people already know (the Slack-like direction of E-002): a sidebar of channels, Activity as a view holding the feed and the history tabs, the conversation in a pane beside it. Each history tab shows a count of its unread entries and marks them. Commits us to reusing those habits and to showing the model inside a familiar frame → [record](D-019-look-and-feel-direction.md)
- **D-020** [accepted] The reason line on a feed row opens why it is there and what to change: what the item is, what put it in the bucket, where it would go otherwise, and one button. It never reads the row; a change from a row acts at once on the narrowest thing, says so and offers Undo → [record](D-020-a-row-explains-its-place.md)
- **D-021** [accepted] Direct messages reach Focus through a list of people that fills itself: an option adds everyone who writes to you directly; switching it off stops adding and asks about those already added; removing from a row removes that person only, without asking. Supersedes the single starting rule for direct messages in D-001 → [record](D-021-focus-people-for-direct-messages.md)
- **D-022** [accepted] Notifications are shown in a separate mock notification app for a selected person, not inside the product: no bell, no tray. Supersedes the tray in D-009; what notifies stays a setting beside the feed settings → [record](D-022-notifications-shown-outside-the-product.md)
- **D-023** [accepted] How the feed is used day to day in the chosen layout: react to any message with a full picker, and from an unopened row; a row has no opened state: a click opens the conversation, beside the feed on a wide screen and over it on a phone; arrivals wait behind a button beside the Activity title; one settings screen, with general rules on the Activity feed page and people and channel levels as lists under it, where anything removed can be added back; a thread in Focus is the thread's own level and is not listed in settings; no inline threads: replies open as their own thread from a link under the message, beside the channel on a wide screen; panels can be resized; the person's own menu in the sidebar; Activity can be closed; on a phone a React button on the row and two swipes, Keep and Mark read. Supersedes expanding in place and replying from the row (D-003). A few further points are the agent's proposals inside the record → [record](D-023-feed-interaction-details.md)
- **D-024** [accepted] How you write (mention suggestions, edit in place, a delete that asks first), what going wrong looks like (offline, a message waiting or failed, search behind, each said in place without moving anything), what search results say, and the two pages outside the product: the control panel and the notification app. Commits us to those forms; the timings behind them belong to D-007 → [record](D-024-writing-failure-states-search-and-outside-pages.md)

## System shape: components, boundaries, stack

- **D-005** [accepted] Parts that talk only over the network and run as separate programs from one codebase, with no message broker; PostgreSQL in Docker as the one source of truth with a numbered change log; a delivery worker per receiver; WebSocket with numbered updates and catch-up to the screen. Commits us to network boundaries between parts and one log every receiver is fed from. The server language (TypeScript) is superseded by D-025. When a broker would become right is recorded in the decision → [record](D-005-system-shape.md)
- **D-025** [accepted] The server is written in Go, the web app in TypeScript, and the contracts are the source both sides are generated from or checked against. Supersedes the server language in D-005. Commits us to two toolchains and a generation step → [record](D-025-server-in-go.md)
- **D-026** [accepted] The architecture is ready for many organizations in one installation (multi-tenant): the organization is part of every key and contract, every organization has its own numbered change log, and the database keeps organizations apart. Organizations are independent, so scaling is spreading them. Managing organizations, an organization switcher, sign-in and running several copies are not built → [record](D-026-ready-for-many-organizations.md)
- **D-017** [accepted] Conversations owns what a reader did (read positions, watching, dials, Focus rules and people, Keep and Done) as facts on the one log; the feed holds nothing original and is worked out from it. Commits us to a feed that can be wiped, fenced and rebuilt, and to every reader action being a round trip through the log → [record](D-017-who-owns-reader-state.md)

## Contracts and qualities: what services promise each other

- **D-018** [accepted] The feed decides that a notification is due and writes it to its own numbered log; the sender only delivers, once per named notification, holding by the log's own clock. Commits us to the sender standing behind the feed, not beside it (a stated departure from the brief's picture), and to notifications waiting while the feed is down → [record](D-018-who-decides-a-notification.md)
- **D-006** [accepted; decided by the agent on the user's delegation, 2026-11-22] Nothing is lost once saved; a change may be repeated; each receiver is fed in log order by its own worker, which waits out an outage and parks a refused change; receivers ignore repeats and older versions, and a delete wins → [record](D-006-delivery-promise.md)
- **D-007** [accepted; decided by the agent on the user's delegation, 2026-11-22] Targets with the failure that proves each: 2 s to search and the feed, a 5 s hold before a notification, 3 s per call, never giving up on an outage, a notice on any surface more than 10 s behind, rebuild in under a minute → [record](D-007-quality-targets.md)
- **D-008** [accepted; decided by the agent on the user's delegation, 2026-11-22] An agent calls us and reads events from its own place; it is told what addresses it; streamed text stays off the reliable path until finished; a cap of three agents in a row and 20 messages a minute → [record](D-008-agent-door.md)
