# D-036: The Activity number counts what would notify you, and a quiet dot shows the rest

- Status: accepted
- Level: product
- Date: 2026-11-23
- Decided by: user
- Depends on: D-012, D-019

## Why this needs deciding now

The number on Activity always added Focus and Needs you. A person who chose "Focus only" was still called by items they had said could wait, so the number contradicted the setting.

## Options

### A. Focus number plus dot

Under Focus only the number counts Focus. A quiet dot appears when Needs you has something unread and Focus has nothing. Calm, and a mention is still noticeable.

### B. Focus number only

The simplest and calmest. A mention can sit unnoticed until the person happens to open Activity.

## Best practice

Work messengers separate a number (addressed to you) from a dot (something new, no rush).

## Recommendation

A. Reversibility: cheap.

## Decision

The user proposed binding the number to the notification setting and chose A.

## Consequences

- The number means one thing: unread rows in the buckets that notify this person. With "Focus and Needs you" nothing changes.
- The sidebar, the phone's bottom bar and the Feed tab show the same mark. Section headers keep their own counts.
- A setting change applies to the mark at once. No contract changes: the screen already receives both counts and the setting.
