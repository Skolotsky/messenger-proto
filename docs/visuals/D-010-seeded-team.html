Decision brief · D-010 · 2026-11-22

Who is in the seeded team, and how busy is it?

The people and agents are scripted and keep working while you watch. This sets who they are, so the product resembles a busy team from the first second.

Needs you

Pick the size of the seeded team. I recommend B: a product startup of 12 people and 3 agents, two weeks before a launch. Names and wording are mine to write; change anything in the cast you like.

The three sizes

A. A handful

6 people, 2 agents, 4 channels.

Upside
Quick to write. Every character is memorable.
Risk
Not busy. Three buckets have little to sort, and agents writing as much as people barely shows.

B. A mid-size startup

Recommended

12 people, 3 agents, 9 channels. About two weeks of history, roughly 1,500 messages, and live activity you can pause, slow down or speed up.

Upside
Busy enough that sorting matters. Mixed roles. Every bucket and dial level has a natural example.
Risk
More to write, and filler text would undermine the craft. The situations are written by hand; only background chatter is generated.

C. A large team

30 or more people, 6 agents, 20 channels.

Upside
The feed is under real pressure.
Risk
Beyond the audience we chose. Nobody can follow the cast. Most effort goes to noise.

The cast in B

12 people
  • Founder
  • Product manager You, by default
  • Designer
  • Three engineers
  • Marketing
  • Sales
  • Customer support
  • Operations and finance
  • Data analyst
  • Part-time contractor
3 agents
  • Release agent. Ships builds and stops for approval.
  • Support agent. Sorts incoming customer issues and flags the ones needing a person.
  • Research agent. Posts progress while it works, then its findings.
9 channels
  • general, product, engineering, design
  • launch, support, incidents
  • sales, random
  • Plus one-to-one direct messages

The switcher shows the same moment as the founder, an engineer or support, so you can see how one message lands differently for each.

Situations the team must produce

Each one happens on its own as the team works, and can be replayed on demand.
