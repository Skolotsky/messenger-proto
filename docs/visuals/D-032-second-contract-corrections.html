Decision brief · D-032 · 2026-11-22

Four choices that change what people see

A second independent check ran real sequences through the contracts and found guarantees that still break. Most fixes are mechanical. These four are yours.

Needs you

Accept the recommended answer on all four? You can also change any single one.

1. Opening a channel

You open #launch. Replies are not shown inline there. Are its threads read too?

A

Recommended

No. Each thread is read when you open it.

Cost
A thread row stays in the feed until opened or marked read.

B

Yes, opening the channel reads its threads.

Risk
Replies you never saw count as read and leave the feed.

2. A reply and the sidebar

Someone replies in a thread in #launch. Does #launch light up in the sidebar?

A

Recommended

No. Replies show in Activity and the Threads tab, the habit people know.

Cost
Someone who ignores Activity can miss a reply.

B

Yes, the channel is marked unread.

Risk
Busy threads keep channels lit, and opening the channel would not clear them.

3. A crash during an agent's reply

The server dies while an agent is writing. What is kept?

A

Recommended

The text so far, saved about once a second. At most the last second is lost.

Cost
One small write per active reply.

B

Nothing: the text lives only in memory.

Risk
The reply is lost, breaking a promise already made.

4. Seeded history and notifications

The team starts with two weeks of history. Does the notification app show it?

A

Recommended

No. It starts empty, so everything in it was really sent during the demo.

Cost
The app looks empty for the first moments.

B

Yes, with a written history of past notifications.

Risk
It shows entries the system never actually sent.

Fixed either way

  • Opening a second view can no longer lose an update.
  • After Reset, screens and agents notice and reload instead of ignoring everything new.
  • A wiped search index is detected and refilled.
  • People and agents can start a direct conversation, which no contract allowed.
