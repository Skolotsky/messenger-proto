Contexia · system map

The whole system on one map

Every program, every database area and every connection. Pick a flow on the right and step through it: the map lights up what happens at that step. The left and right arrow keys work too.

    How to read it

    Top to bottom

    People and agents at the top, the programs in the middle, stored data at the bottom. Screens and agents send their work only to Conversations.

    Left to right

    The originals are on the left. Everything to the right is a copy fed from a log, and can be erased and refilled, except the sender’s list of names already sent.

    What runs today

    In the demonstration, the demo program is the one web address. It serves the pages and passes each call on to the single copy of Conversations. The router that picks between two copies exists only in the tests.
